## Design and content direction

Every change people will see, read or watch must help this project stand out: premium, specific and human, never templated or AI-looking.

- Visual work (pages, components, layouts, styles, emails, graphics): load the `impeccable-design` skill first and follow it.
- Words that ship (UI text, marketing pages, emails, posts, job adverts, SEO titles and meta): load `stop-slop-marketing` first.
- Video, animation and motion: load `hyperframes-video` first.
- The skills live in `.claude/skills/` in this repo and in the account's saved skills.
- This repo's existing theme, tokens and components are the brand source of truth. Reuse them, and add a new palette, font or component library only when asked.
- Check UI on the rendered result at desktop and phone widths, in every state, before calling it done, and say what was checked.
- Never invent claims, ratings, reviews, prices or results in copy or demo content.
